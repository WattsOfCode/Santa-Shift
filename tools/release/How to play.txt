SANTA SHIFT
===========

To play: double-click SantaShift.exe. Nothing else to install.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the game isn't signed.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Controls: WASD or the arrow keys to move, the mouse to look, E to use a station, 1 2 3 at the bench,
Esc to pause. A controller works too. Change any control in Settings > Controls.

Keep the Content folder next to SantaShift.exe: the game loads its models, sounds and data from it.
Your settings, unlocks and best scores are saved in your user folder, so you can delete or replace this
folder without losing them (Settings > Game > Start over erases them).
